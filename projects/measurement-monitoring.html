From a measurement to a monitoring system

Legacy-well methane emissions can vary over time. A brief measurement may miss an intermittent release or misrepresent its typical behavior. This work emphasizes continuous observation, sensor QA, and the environmental context needed to interpret concentration data.

Design priorities

  • Detect low methane signals with a high-precision sensor.
  • Observe intermittent behavior over a meaningful time window.
  • Control or characterize airflow through a ventilation canopy.
  • Maintain consistent data acquisition and timestamping.
  • Separate instrument behavior from changes in the emission source.

Monitoring logic

InstrumentSelect sensor range, resolution, response time, and placement for the intended field condition.
ObserveCollect continuous concentration and system-condition data.
Quality-checkIdentify gaps, drift, inconsistent periods, and signals below a reliable interpretation threshold.
CharacterizeDescribe variability and intermittency rather than reporting only a single average.
