Measurement and Monitoring

Methods for low, variable, and intermittent methane emissions.

Overview

Legacy-well emissions can vary over time. A brief measurement may miss an intermittent release or misrepresent typical behavior. This work emphasizes continuous observation, sensor quality assurance, and the environmental context needed to interpret concentration data.

Design Priorities

  • Detect low methane signals with a high-precision sensor.
  • Observe intermittent behavior over a meaningful time window.
  • Control or characterize airflow through a ventilation canopy.
  • Maintain consistent data acquisition and timestamps.
  • Separate instrument behavior from changes in the emission source.
