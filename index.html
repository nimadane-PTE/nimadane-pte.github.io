Summary

Energy and petroleum engineer with experience in methane emission monitoring, IoT sensor deployment, computational fluid dynamics, machine learning, air-quality research, and atmospheric modeling. My work connects field measurements with physical and data-driven models to improve the understanding of emissions from legacy oil and gas assets and industrial sources.

Focus Areas

Methane detection and monitoring, AI-assisted computational fluid dynamics, low and intermittent emission measurement, legacy-well data, CO₂ measurement, air-quality monitoring, inverse dispersion modeling, CMAQ, SPECIATE, and time-series prediction.
